# Uppcoder

**Build, run and teach software in the cloud.**

Uppcoder is a cloud development platform. Build your stack from the frameworks, databases and services you need,
work in the editor of your choice, and run it on our cloud, with each app on its own address. Or describe your app
in a sentence and the AI builder creates it.

- **Developers and teams:** your stack, your editor, from development to production on one platform.
- **AI builder:** one sentence in, a working app out. [See an order app it built](https://demo.uppcoder.com/order-app).
- **Schools:** every student gets the same ready environment.
- **Hackathons:** every team gets its own environment.

Hosted in Germany. Made by [Websson](https://websson-doo.com).
